## One Core Form, Several Supporting Schedules

Since 2018, the IRS consolidated several old 1040 variants (1040-A, 1040-EZ) into a single **Form 1040**, with additional detail pushed onto numbered schedules only when they apply to you. Most filers with simple W-2 income never touch a schedule at all.

## Form 1040 vs. 1040-SR

**Form 1040-SR** is functionally identical to the standard 1040 — same lines, same calculations — but uses larger print and includes a built-in standard deduction chart, designed for filers **65 and older**. You can use either form at that age; 1040-SR exists purely for readability, not different tax treatment.

## The Core Schedules You Might Need

| Schedule | Used For |
|---|---|
| Schedule 1 | Additional income (unemployment, gambling) and adjustments (student loan interest, educator expenses) |
| Schedule 2 | Additional taxes (AMT, excess advance premium tax credit repayment, self-employment tax) |
| Schedule 3 | Additional credits and payments (foreign tax credit, education credits, retirement savings credit) |
| Schedule A | Itemized deductions |
| Schedule B | Interest and dividend income over $1,500 |
| Schedule C | Profit or loss from a sole proprietorship or freelance business |
| Schedule D | Capital gains and losses |
| Schedule E | Rental income, royalties, partnerships, S-corps |
| Schedule SE | Self-employment tax calculation |

## A Typical W-2 Employee's Return

Someone with only wage income, the standard deduction, and no side income needs just the core two-page Form 1040 — no schedules at all. Add a side gig reported on a 1099-NEC, and Schedule C (business profit/loss) and Schedule SE (self-employment tax) both become necessary.

## Reading Your Own 1040

The form flows in a specific order worth understanding:
1. **Income** (lines 1–9): wages, interest, dividends, capital gains, business income, Social Security
2. **Adjustments** (Schedule 1, Part II): subtracted to reach AGI
3. **AGI** (line 11)
4. **Deduction** (line 12): standard or itemized
5. **Taxable income** (line 15): AGI minus deduction
6. **Tax** (line 16): computed from the tax tables or brackets
7. **Credits** (lines 19–21): Child Tax Credit, education credits, etc.
8. **Other taxes** (Schedule 2): self-employment tax, NIIT
9. **Payments** (lines 25–33): withholding, estimated payments, refundable credits
10. **Refund or amount owed** (lines 34–38)

## Common Attachments Beyond Schedules

- **Form W-2** — reported, not attached, but the withholding on it flows to line 25a
- **Form 1099** series — report income from freelance work, interest, dividends, retirement distributions
- **Form 8812** — calculates the Child Tax Credit and Additional Child Tax Credit
- **Form 8863** — education credits
- **Form 2441** — child and dependent care expenses
